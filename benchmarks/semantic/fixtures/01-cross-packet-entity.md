# Meridian Research Ledger

Meridian Research Cooperative was founded in 2018. The organization is also called MRC in its
public reports.

## Programs

MRC operates the Atlas evidence program. Meridian Research Cooperative remains the legal name.

## Governance

The Cooperative appointed Lin Tao as director in 2024. References to Meridian Research, MRC, and
the Cooperative in this document identify the same organization.
